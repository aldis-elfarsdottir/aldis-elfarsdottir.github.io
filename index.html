About

As an Instructor at Stanford University with a PhD from Stanford Management Science & Engineering, I combine over five years of applied research and teaching experience with a focus on data science and sustainability. My work integrates the analysis of large-scale text data and emissions forecasting models, facilitating organizations' efforts to meet climate and sustainability goals.
